Privacy policy

Chief of staff · Last updated 2026-11-19

Who uses this app

Chief of staff is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app may access:

  • Gmail messages, threads, labels and drafts in the owner's account;
  • Google Calendar calendars and events in the owner's account;
  • the account's email address, to tell the owner's accounts apart.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide features the owner asks for: summarizing and sorting mail, saving draft replies, sending email the owner requested, and reading and updating the owner's calendar. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to build profiles of anyone.

Where the data goes

Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's computer, in its secure keychain and local files. To carry out a request, the app may send the relevant content to the AI assistant the owner uses on that computer to process that request. Data is not shared with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Limited Use

Chief of staff's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Notes the app writes stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all further access.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the user support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
