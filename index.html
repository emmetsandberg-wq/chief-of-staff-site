Chief of staff

A private assistant that helps its owner manage their own email and calendar.

Chief of staff runs on its owner's own computer. With the owner's permission, it connects to the owner's Google accounts to read and organize their email, save draft replies, send messages the owner asks for, and keep their calendar in order.

It is a personal tool, not a public service. There are no other users, no sign-ups and no accounts to create. Only the people who run their own copy of it, on their own computer, with their own Google accounts, use it.

What it does with Google

  • Gmail: reads the owner's mail to summarize it and find what needs attention, saves drafts, and sends email the owner has asked for.
  • Google Calendar: reads the owner's calendars and adds or changes events the owner asks for.

See the privacy policy for how this data is handled.
